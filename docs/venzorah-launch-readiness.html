VenzorahLaunch readiness · 22 October 2026

Where the project stands

Venzorah is one Next.js application. The shop, the customer account, the vendor dashboard, the admin desk and the database rules all live in this repository. There is no separate backend still to be written.

The site is deployed and the domain answers. A visitor can browse, create an account, and walk through checkout. That checkout does not charge a real card. Vendor earnings are calculated and stored, and an administrator can approve a withdrawal on the ledger. The money is not sent to a bank.

Safe to say today

  • The product can be demonstrated to partners.
  • Staff, buyers and vendors each have a working area.
  • Prices, commission and shipping are calculated on the server in pence.
  • The rules for a multi-vendor order are written and tested.

Hold the public launch

  • No live card charge and no bank payout.
  • The production database still has to be confirmed on Vercel.
  • Email is recorded, and it is not delivered.
  • Terms, privacy and returns are still placeholder text.

4. How an order is divided

These rules are in the application and covered by tests. They apply when a payment is confirmed. They do not move money at a bank.

  • One checkout, one order per seller. A basket with two sellers becomes a parent order and a sub-order for each seller.
  • Shipping stays with the seller who charged it. The platform does not take a share of the delivery fee.
  • Commission is on goods only. The seeded Free plan is 5% of eligible product sales, after any discount share. An active paid plan is 0% while it covers the sale. Seeded paid prices are £35 a month, £180 for six months and £336 a year. Staff can change plans.
  • Free plan listings. The seeded Free plan allows 5 published listings. Paid plans are unlimited unless staff set a cap.
  • Seven-day wait. Each sub-order becomes available after seven days, unless that seller has an open dispute. The wait is a setting, and a scheduled job has to run for the release to happen.
  • Refunds. A product refund reverses the commission on that product amount. Shipping is refunded only when staff refund shipping on purpose. A dispute refund does not automatically refund the delivery fee.
  • The books balance. Amounts are whole pence. A checkout that cannot be reconciled is refused. Paying the same order twice does not write a second payment.
Worked example, already in the tests. Seller A sells £30 of goods and charges £5 delivery on the Free plan: commission £1.50, seller £33.50. Seller B sells £20 and charges £3.50 on a paid plan: commission £0, seller £23.50. The buyer pays £58.50. Venzorah’s commission is £1.50. The £8.50 of delivery is not platform income.

5. Required before the public can trade

Do these in roughly this order. A public announcement before they are done would take orders the platform cannot collect or pay out.

WorkWhy it blocks launch
01 Required
Production database		 Set a remote PostgreSQL address on Vercel for Production and Preview, and include it in the build. Use the direct address, not a pooled host. A localhost address is ignored on purpose. Without this, new accounts and real orders on the live site have nowhere reliable to live. Then run one signup and one order on that database and confirm they are still there after a new deploy.
02 Required
Real card payments		 Checkout currently accepts the public test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 inside Venzorah’s own form. No charge is made. Stripe keys are empty, and the Stripe webhook answers that it is not configured. Replace that form with Stripe Checkout or Stripe Elements so the card number never reaches Venzorah. Point the webhook at the live site and confirm a paid order only after Stripe says it is paid.
03 Required
Paid seller plans		 Choosing Monthly, 6-Month or Annual records the plan. It does not take the fee. A seller must be charged, through Stripe or another provider, before the 0% commission rate applies in production.
04 Required
How sellers get paid		 Withdrawals are requests on an internal ledger. Staff can approve them. There is no Stripe Connect account and no automatic transfer to a seller’s bank. Choose the payout method, write it into the seller terms, and rehearse one payout from approval to money leaving Venzorah.
05 Required
The daily job		 Earnings leave the seven-day wait only when POST /api/cron/jobs runs. Subscriptions expire and reminders run in the same job. Nothing in the Vercel project calls it yet. Put it on a daily schedule and set a real CRON_SECRET. If that secret is left empty, the job does not check who is calling it.
06 Required
Email that arrives		 Password reset, order mail and seller notices are written to the database. They are not sent. Filling in an SMTP host still only marks the message as queued. Connect a real sender and receive one reset email, one order email and one seller email in a real inbox.

VenzorahLaunch sequence

7. A practical order of work

  1. Database and secrets. Remote Postgres, a new staff password, a cron secret, and the public site address.
  2. Stripe for buyers. Live or test keys, hosted card entry, and a webhook that marks the order paid.
  3. Stripe for seller plans. The fee is collected before the 0% commission applies.
  4. Payout decision. Bank transfer by staff, or a connected-account product. Say which one in the seller agreement.
  5. Email. A sender that delivers, tested with a real inbox.
  6. Daily job. Earnings, expired plans and reminders run every day.
  7. Storage. Photos and identity documents survive a new deploy and stay private where they must.
  8. Legal and VAT. Replace the placeholder pages and confirm the receipt.
  9. Identity checks and the first real sellers.
  10. Switch off demonstration sign-in, the test card and the fallback catalogue.
  11. Dress rehearsal, then open.

8. What “launched” should mean

Ready to demonstrate

This is the project today. Partners can click through the shop, the three dashboards and a demonstration payment on venzorah.co.uk, once the database is attached. Say that it is a demonstration.

Ready to trade

A stranger can pay, a seller is checked, the fee and the commission match the contract, the earning is released by the daily job, and the seller can be paid. Items 01 to 10 in section 5 are the gate.

What this document is. It describes the application as it stands on 22 October 2026. It is a delivery and launch list. It is not a legal opinion, a VAT opinion, or a payment-provider contract. Prices of plans can be changed by staff. The commission and shipping rules above are the rules in the code.
